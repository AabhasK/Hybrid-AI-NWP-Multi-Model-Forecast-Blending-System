How this is measured

Scores come from contiguous time-block cross-validation, not a random split: neighbouring days and cells are strongly correlated, so a random split leaks the answer across the fold boundary and inflates every number. Every row is predicted by a model that never saw its block.

The baseline that matters is the equal-weight multi-model mean — averaging every source with no learning at all. It is a famously stubborn benchmark, so it is reported above rather than quietly omitted.
